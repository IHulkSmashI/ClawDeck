Tabs in Quick Access.

- **Tabs you switch with LB and RB:** ClawDeck's Quick Access panel now has four tabs always on screen, Power, Game,
  Fixes and Setup, instead of sections you open and close. LB and RB move between them, like the tabs in Steam's
  Library, and ClawDeck remembers the tab you were on. Thanks Rafalskyy for the idea, and unifideck for showing how
  Steam's own tabs fit in Quick Access.

**Update:** Setup → **Update to 0.2.0 beta 18** (from beta 3 or later). Otherwise Quick Access → Decky → gear →
Developer → **Install plugin from URL**:
`https://github.com/IHulkSmashI/ClawDeck/releases/download/v0.2.0-beta.18/ClawDeck.zip`.
