Battery and GPU watts, emulators, the temperature guard and a diagnostics log, all from anaveto.

- **Battery watts:** the Claw 8 EX reports its battery current wrongly (about −64 A), and ClawDeck used to divide it
  by 100, which read about 30 % low. It now takes back the real value, so "Battery now" on the Fixes page is right.
- **GPU watts in the overlay:** "Show watts in the overlay" now also opens the counter for the graphics part of the
  chip. MangoHud versions with flightlessmango/MangoHud#2211 show it as GPU watts instead of 0.0 W.
- **Emulators:** autotune stays out of xemu, which shows 60 fps whatever the game's real speed, so your own limit stays
  on the chip. Games without their own MangoHud log (emulators from ES-DE, for one) now get their frame rate from
  Steam's overlay.
- **Temperature guard:** when a couple of busy cores make the chip hot while it's well under its power limit (xemu
  does this), the guard now lowers the CPU's top clock instead of cutting the watts for everything. Watts steps now
  start from what the chip actually draws.
- **Diagnostics log** (Setup, off by default): one row a second of power, temperature, battery, clocks and fans, plus
  the controller's reports, so a bug report shows the seconds before a problem. Kept 30 days, at most 200 MB.

Thanks anaveto for all of these!
