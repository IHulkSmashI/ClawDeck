Autotune fixes from anaveto, and the newest XeSS XMX package.

- **Switching Autotune off now gives the watts back:** switching Autotune off in a game, or changing something under
  Advanced, used to leave Autotune's last (lower) TDP on the chip until the game ended. Your pick now goes back at
  once, and your own change stays.
- **Temperature guard:** after the chip cooled down, the TDP now comes back up in games Autotune stays out of (xemu,
  or with Autotune switched off). It used to stay at the guard's lower step.
- **xemu:** picking a new Autotune target in an xemu game no longer makes Autotune take over the game.
- **"Set PL2 yourself"** now reads the value back from the chip. The toast shows the watts, and it says so when the
  chip didn't take the value.
- **Autotune's log** (`autotune.csv`) now has frame times (median, 95th percentile, slow frames) for games whose frame
  rate comes from Steam's overlay. A limit that's only a bit too low shows up there as slow frames even when the fps
  looks full.
- **XeSS XMX package 20261115:** lower latency with frame generation. HHHHanasak1 measured Cyberpunk 2077 at 3x going
  from 45 to 32 ms, and CONTROL Resonant at 3x from 53 to 48 ms. Setup → **Update XeSS XMX**.

Thanks anaveto for all of these!
