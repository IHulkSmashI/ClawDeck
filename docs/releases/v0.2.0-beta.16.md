Non-Steam games with their own Proton prefix, and the older Capcom games.

- **Non-Steam games with a custom prefix:** when a shortcut's launch options send Proton to its own prefix
  (`STEAM_COMPAT_DATA_PATH=...`), ClawDeck now always uses that one. Steam creates an empty `compatdata` folder for the
  shortcut at its first launch, and ClawDeck looked there instead, so the Game page said the XMX cores were never used
  and the health check looked in the wrong place. XeSS on XMX itself kept working. A path starting with `~` or `$HOME`
  works too (thanks maltyjava for tracking it down!).
- **Capcom games without DLSS:** Resident Evil 2, 3, 4 (2023), 7 and Village, Devil May Cry 5 and Street Fighter 6
  have no DLSS in the game, so ClawDeck's Capcom setup (it swaps the game's DLSS and DLSS frame generation for XeSS
  on the XMX cores) had nothing to work with there: RE4 ran as if nothing was installed. ClawDeck no longer installs
  it in these games and the Game page says why; **Uninstall OptiScaler** takes out a setup from an earlier beta.
  Proper support for them, through REFramework's pd-upscaler build, comes once it's tested on a Claw (thanks
  Kisenir!).
