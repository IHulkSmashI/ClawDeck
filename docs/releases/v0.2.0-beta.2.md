A small update to the first beta.

- **FSR 4.1.2 comes inside ClawDeck now.** One install, nothing extra to download: the build packages the files
  (each one checked), and ClawDeck puts them in place by itself.
- ClawDeck checks which handheld it's on first and only shows what that one can do. Chips without XMX cores (Claw
  A1M, A8) skip the XMX driver and FSR 4.1.2. The ONEXPLAYER 3 is in as a beta device (power, fan, charge limit,
  and a brightness control that works on its OLED).

**Install or update:** Quick Access → Decky → gear → Developer → **Install plugin from URL**, and enter
`https://github.com/IHulkSmashI/ClawDeck/releases/download/v0.2.0-beta.2/ClawDeck.zip`.

Everything else, known limits and feedback links: see
[v0.2.0-beta.1](https://github.com/IHulkSmashI/ClawDeck/releases/tag/v0.2.0-beta.1) and the
[README](https://github.com/IHulkSmashI/ClawDeck#readme).
