Fans, autotune and ES-DE from anaveto, lower frame generation latency, and a gyro fix.

- **Lower latency with XeSS frame generation:** XeSS XMX package 20261113. HHHHanasak1 reports CONTROL Resonant at 3x
  on the Claw 8 EX's chip going from about 35 ms to about 23 ms. Setup → **Update XeSS XMX** after updating ClawDeck.
- **Fans** (thanks anaveto!): presets now reach the fans exactly (Quiet's 75 % used to land at 74 %, and the panel
  then said the preset didn't apply). A new **Extreme** preset sits between Cooling and Full speed, and the panel shows
  the curve the fans follow right now.
- **Autotune** (thanks anaveto!): MangoHud's frame-time log starts again, so autotune reads the frame rate in games
  without XeSS. It only reads the running game's log, and when it can't get a frame rate it puts your own limit back
  instead of leaving its starting point on the chip.
- **ES-DE** (thanks anaveto!): each game you start from ES-DE gets its own power pick and autotune, instead of one
  limit for NES and PS3 alike. Turn it on in Fixes → ES-DE.
- **Smaller things:** the PL2 slider says whether it's the plugged-in or battery value, the Game section only shows the
  running game or the one you pick, and the temperature guard logs temperature, power and fan speed to `thermal.csv`.
- **Gyro on the Claw 8 EX** (thanks chris-hmmr for tracking it down!): on newer Bazzite kernels the gyro never came up.
  ClawDeck now puts MSI's sensor-hub firmware into the boot image and adds the rule that loads the gyro program. If you
  installed the gyro before, ClawDeck fixes it by itself: restart once, then shut down fully and turn the Claw on again.
- **Capcom games:** REFramework release 19 of onehoon's fork. Press **Install** again on the game's page to get it.
