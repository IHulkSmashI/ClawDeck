**ClawDeck: a free Decky plugin that runs FSR 4.1.2, XeSS and XeSS frame gen on Intel's XMX cores (MSI Claw, Bazzite)**

If you've got an Intel MSI Claw on Bazzite, we just put out the first beta of ClawDeck:
https://github.com/IHulkSmashI/ClawDeck

It's one Quick Access panel with:

- FSR 4.1.2 and XeSS 2 running on the XMX (AI) cores, set up per game in one tap
- XeSS frame gen 2x, 3x or 4x with low latency on
- Power profiles, PL2, fan curves, charge limit and an Autotune beta
- The fixes the Claw needs on Linux: VRR from 30 Hz, the MSI buttons, real watts in the overlay

Tested on a Claw 8 EX. Open source (AGPLv3), feedback very welcome on GitHub.
